Wardrobe

Leif is a tiny leaf spirit who has been looking after Agatha's old greenhouse tower all on his own for a very long time. Grow plants, build your tower floor by floor, and keep him company.

  • Grow, collect and sell 50 hand-pixeled plants
  • Build greenhouses, a Plant Shop and a cozy Cafe
  • Decorate every room and dress Leif up in hats
  • Meet the regulars who come looking for the perfect plant
